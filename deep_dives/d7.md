## Organizational evangelism & alignment

An engineering team finds out the roadmap changed when a ticket they'd already scoped gets pulled. A sales rep learns about a repositioning when a prospect quotes the old pitch back at them. Product direction is real, but nobody outside the PM function seems to know about it until it's already affecting their work — alignment gets rebuilt one one-on-one conversation at a time, over and over.

This dimension measures whether product direction propagates through the organization as a system, or leaks out through whoever happens to ask the PM directly.

### Where most organizations start (Nascent)

Product direction is communicated reactively and inconsistently — teams learn about roadmap decisions when those decisions affect their work, not before. The first real step is a recurring communication cadence with a consistent format, defining the minimum each function — Engineering, GTM, Leadership — actually needs to know.

### Where the real gains happen (Modeled → Integral)

The real shift isn't a better newsletter — it's differentiating the message by audience, so Engineering gets specification-level context, GTM gets positioning-level context, and Leadership gets portfolio-level context, each derived from the same underlying artifacts rather than independently authored. From there, the meaningful gain is moving from event-driven to continuous propagation: direction artifacts update as the PM's own source artifacts update, and — crucially — the system starts flagging misalignment proactively, catching a team drifting from current direction before that drift becomes an expensive handoff failure.

### What the top of the curve actually looks like (Telemetric)

At full maturity, product direction is continuously and automatically propagated across the organization at the fidelity each audience needs, all derived from the same underlying artifacts without manual reformulation — and misalignment is surfaced proactively, not discovered downstream when the damage is already done.

### Why this dimension matters

This dimension isn't about AI depth the way most of the others are — it tracks organizational reach and coherence, which is a different kind of maturity entirely. A PM function can have a brilliant, well-reasoned roadmap and still watch it fail in practice, simply because the rest of the organization found out too late or heard a version that had already drifted from what was actually decided.
